About QuickNotes

QuickNotes provides a simple, organized space for users to create, edit, and manage their notes digitally.
It is designed to make capturing and organizing information quick, convenient, and accessible.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your note in the field under "Add a note".
  2. Click add note to save your note.
  3. View your notes in the 'Your Note' section.

Features

  • Create Notes - Type and Save new notes.
  • View Notes - All your saved notes are displayed for easy access.
